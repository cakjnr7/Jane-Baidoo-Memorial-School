Our approach

Learning with purpose and wonder.

School Background

Jane Baidoo Memorial School was established to provide a nurturing educational environment rooted in respect, meaningful learning, and personal growth.

Vision

To nurture confident, compassionate, and capable learners who are prepared to contribute positively to society.

Mission

To provide a safe, engaging, and intellectually stimulating environment where every learner can thrive academically, socially, and emotionally.

Core Values

Respect, integrity, responsibility, curiosity, perseverance, and excellence guide the school community.

School Philosophy

We believe children learn best when they feel secure, respected, and genuinely inspired to investigate and explore.

Educational Objectives

To foster academic excellence, character development, creativity, collaboration, and a lifelong love for learning.

Academic structure

Clear learning pathways from early years to senior secondary.

Early Years

Nursery & Reception

Ages 3–5

Foundational learning through play, language development, early numeracy, sensory exploration, and social-emotional growth.

Lower Primary

Classes 1–3

Ages 6–8

Structured literacy, numeracy, science, creative arts, and essential life skills that build confidence and independence.

Upper Primary

Classes 4–6

Ages 9–11

Expanded academic learning, deeper subject knowledge, project-based activities, and growing responsibility for self-directed study.

Junior Secondary

JHS 1–3

Ages 12–14

Academic rigour with a broad curriculum covering core subjects, creative expression, practical work, and personal development.

Senior Secondary

SHS 1–3

Ages 15–17

Focused preparation for higher education, careers, and life readiness through stronger academic specialization and guided mentorship.

Admissions

Simple steps to joining our school community.

Admission process

  1. Parents or guardians make an initial enquiry about admission for the child.
  2. Obtain and complete the official admission application form.
  3. Submit the completed form to the school office or designated contact point.
  4. Provide all required supporting documents such as birth certificate, previous school records, and identification.
  5. Complete any required assessment, screening, or interview as applicable.
  6. Receive admission confirmation and notification from the school.
  7. Pay the applicable admission fees and any required charges.
  8. Complete enrolment formalities and prepare for resumption.

Financial aid

Support opportunities may be available depending on school policy and eligibility. Families seeking assistance should make their enquiry early and provide the required documentation for review.

  • Scholarship or assistance opportunities as announced by the school
  • Eligibility and need-based review through the school office
  • Application supported by required family and student records
  • Assessment and approval coordinated with the admissions team
  • Further enquiries: admin@jbm.edu.gh
